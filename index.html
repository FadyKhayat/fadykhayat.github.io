About me

I’m a 3D Artist & Creative Director. I help brands and agencies turn ideas, products and spaces into compelling 3D visuals, from photoreal CGI ads to navigable virtual tours built from floor plans. Before moving into 3D, I worked as a designer at M&C Saatchi and built my own clothing brand, so I think about the idea and the message as much as the render.

I believe great CGI goes beyond aesthetics. It should serve a purpose and leave a lasting impression. I handle everything myself, from the first creative direction to the final grade, with clear communication and realistic timelines.
