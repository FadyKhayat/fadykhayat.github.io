About me

I started in graphic design and video editing, worked at M&C Saatchi and built my own clothing brand, then moved into 3D and VR. That mix is why I think about the idea and the brand as much as the render.

Today I work mostly in Blender, with ZBrush, Substance Painter, Unreal Engine 5 and Marvelous Designer when a project calls for them. I build, light and finish everything myself, from the first block-out to the final grade.
